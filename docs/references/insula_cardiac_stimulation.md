# fMOST references: insula and cardiac stimulation

Added: 2026-11-23 (Asia/Shanghai). Bibliographic metadata and abstracts checked against the linked PubMed and publisher records.

These papers provide functional and autonomic background for fMOST interpretation within VisPa. Their relevance to this project is interpretive; they do not describe implemented VisPa methods or use fMOST to establish the reported cardiac responses.

[Import all three references as BibTeX](insula_cardiac_stimulation.bib) · [VisPa insula study workflow](../project_note.md#insula-study-workflow)

## 1. Human insular stimulation — Chouchou et al. (2019)

Chouchou, F., Mauguière, F., Vallayer, O., Catenoix, H., Isnard, J., Montavont, A., Jung, J., Pichot, V., Rheims, S., & Mazzola, L. (2019). **How the insula speaks to the heart: Cardiac responses to insular stimulation in humans.** *Human Brain Mapping, 40*(9), 2611–2622.

- DOI: [10.1002/hbm.24548](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.24548).
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30815964/) · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6865697/).
- **Evidence:** Intracranial electrical stimulation of the insula in people with epilepsy produced both bradycardia and tachycardia.
- **Project relevance (interpretation):** Supports considering cardiac/autonomic function when forming hypotheses about insular subregions and their anatomical projections.

## 2. Broader human autonomic network — Chouchou et al. (2025)

Chouchou, F., Soulier, H., Pichot, V., Mauguière, F., Faillenot, I., Guénot, M., Hermier, M., Jung, J., Montavont, A., Catenoix, H., Isnard, J., Roche, F., Rheims, S., & Mazzola, L. (2025). **Cardiac autonomic responses to cortical electrical stimulation: a SEEG study.** *NeuroImage, 318*, 121423.

- DOI: [10.1016/j.neuroimage.2025.121423](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2025.121423).
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40819828/) · [Open-access publisher article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811925004264).
- **Evidence:** SEEG electrical stimulation in people with epilepsy mapped cardiac responses across a broader network, including posterior insula, amygdala, anterior cingulate, and medial frontal regions.
- **Project relevance (interpretation):** Provides a functional comparison for candidate autonomic target domains in insula projectome analyses.

## 3. Rhesus macaque mediodorsal thalamus — Méndez et al. (2023)

Méndez, J. C., Perry, B. A. L., Premereur, E., Pelekanos, V., Ramadan, T., & Mitchell, A. S. (2023). **Variable cardiac responses in rhesus macaque monkeys after discrete mediodorsal thalamus manipulations.** *Scientific Reports, 13*(1), 16913.

- DOI: [10.1038/s41598-023-42752-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-42752-4).
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37805650/) · [Open-access publisher article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-42752-4) · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10560229/).
- **Evidence:** Manipulation of the mediodorsal thalamus in rhesus macaques was associated with cardiac changes. The intervention target was **mediodorsal thalamus, not insula**.
- **Project relevance (interpretation):** Supplies nonhuman-primate thalamic/autonomic context for discussing insular and wider brain–heart circuits.

## Interpretation boundary

Electrical-stimulation responses do not identify the projection route or the cardiac role of an individual fMOST-reconstructed neuron. Species, intervention target, recording conditions, and stimulation protocols must remain explicit in comparisons. This addition records references and scoped relevance notes; it does not adopt a stimulation protocol or change anatomical labels, analysis methods, or scientific acceptance.

Full Methods were not systematically reviewed for this reference addition. BibTeX contains one record per DOI; 121423 and 16913 are article numbers stored in the conventional `pages` field for citation-tool compatibility.
